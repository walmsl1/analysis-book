Theorem 0.6.1 The \(\epsilon\)–\(\delta\) property.

Let \(a\in\operatorname{dom}(f)\text{.}\) The function \(f\) is continuous at \(a\) if and only if \(\forall\,\epsilon\gt 0\ \exists\,\delta\gt 0\) s.t. \(\forall\, x\in\operatorname{dom}(f)\) with \(|x-a|\lt\delta\text{,}\) \(|f(x)-f(a)|\lt\epsilon\text{.}\)
